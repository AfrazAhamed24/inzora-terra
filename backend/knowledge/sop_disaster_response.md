# SOP: Disaster Response Fundamentals

## Earthquake Response Protocol
- **Immediate evacuation** for HIGH risk zones
- **Search and rescue** in priority zones using thermal and GPR
- **Monitor for secondary collapse** after initial event
- **Tilt > 2 degrees** requires structural assessment before entry

## Building Collapse Assessment
- **Assess structural integrity** before any entry
- **Monitor for secondary collapse** during operations
- **Establish 100m perimeter** around affected structure
- **Only authorized teams** with proper equipment may enter

## Victim Search Procedures
- **Use thermal imaging** to detect heat signatures
- **Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR)** for void Spaces
- **Prioritize high-probability zones** first (probability > 0.6)
- **Continuous reassessment** as new evidence arrives
