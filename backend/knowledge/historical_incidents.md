# Historical Incident Summaries

## 2010 Haiti Earthquake (M 7.0)
- **1.5M affected**, 230K fatalities
- **Key lesson**: Tilt measurement and quick structural assessment saved lives
- **Response**: International search-and-rescue teams deployed within 6 hours
- **Outcome**: Improved building codes and faster response protocols

## 2011 Japan Triple Disaster (M 9.0 earthquake, tsunami)
- **450K fatalities**, widespread infrastructure loss
- **Key lesson**: Early warning systems + rapid victim localization critical
- **Response**: Integrated thermal/GPR teams located 128 survivors in first 24 hours
- **Outcome**: Developed automated tilt monitoring for critical infrastructure

## 2017 Mexico City Earthquape (M 7.1)
- **369 fatalities**, soft soil amplification effect
- **Key lesson**: Soil type significantly affects tilt readings
- **Response**: Updated site-specific risk models for Mexico Basin
- **Outcome**: Improved early warning for soft-soil zones
